# Solana Lab Default Commercial Policy v0.1

## Source policy
Solana Lab is public source-visible but proprietary by default.

Public visibility is for review, demos, ecosystem evaluation and technical
transparency. It is not an open-source grant.

## Product use is not source licensing
A user may pay to use a running Solana Lab product without obtaining rights to
copy, fork, self-host, modify, redistribute, deploy or commercialize the code.

## Risk Passport initial price hypothesis
**USD 0.50 per paid production scan/proof.**

This price is not hard-coded in Core or Attestation Engine. It belongs to the
Risk Passport billing layer so pricing may change without redeploying
foundation programs.

## Free evaluation layer
Where explicitly offered:
- source inspection;
- public demos;
- devnet testing;
- limited evaluation;
- event / hackathon demonstration.

## Relicensing
Solana Foundation, Superteam or another ecosystem sponsor may justify
different terms for a selected module. Any relicensing must be explicit,
versioned and module-specific.
